Complex

Complex is a self-contained pack of photo pages holding one to five photos. It offers asymmetric compositions, captions and an automatic mode that chooses a layout according to the orientation of the photos and the page.

Choosing a template

In Album settings, open the photo-page section and select a Complex template. Its settings button gives access to the layout, when several variants are available, and to captions. From Plan, you can also change the template of an individual page without changing the album-wide choice.

These templates are intended for photo pages in the album body. The pack does not provide covers or dividers. Images keep their proportions: they are fitted into their slots without cropping. Captions can show the date and time, location and custom text, with controls for the font, size, color and order of the caption elements.

One photo

One photo — A single image occupies the photographic area of the page, with its caption. This template accepts a portrait, landscape or square photo on any of these page shapes. It is also the automatic mode’s final fallback.

Two photos

Two photos — On a portrait or square page, the images are placed one above the other. On a landscape page, they are side by side. Automatic mode pairs two landscape photos in the first case, or two portrait photos in the second. Manual selection remains possible with other orientations, which may leave more unused space around the images.

Three photos

Three photos — One large image is accompanied by two smaller ones. The two variants place the large photo first or last in the sequence:

  • Portrait or square page: large photo above, two smaller photos below; or two smaller photos above, large photo below.
  • Landscape page: large photo on the left, two smaller photos on the right; or two smaller photos on the left, large photo on the right.

Four photos — Reportage

Four photos — Reportage compositions combine one portrait and three landscape photos. On a portrait or landscape page, one landscape is larger than the other two. On a square page, three equally sized landscapes form a column beside the portrait. Four variants are available on a portrait page, four on a landscape page and two on a square page.

Depending on the page shape, the layout changes the position of the portrait, the side occupied by the small images, or the position of the large landscape above or below them. In automatic mode, the portrait can occupy any of the four chronological positions on a portrait page. On a landscape or square page, it must be the first or fourth photo in the group.

Five photos — Mosaic and Sequence

Five photos brings two composition families together in one template:

  • Mosaic — Three portrait and two landscape photos. On a portrait page, a column of two portraits accompanies a column of one portrait and two landscapes, on the left or right; the portrait in the triple column can be at the top, in the middle or at the bottom. On a landscape page, three columns containing two portraits, one portrait and two landscapes are permuted. On a square page, a row of two portraits and one landscape accompanies a row of one portrait and one landscape, above or below it; orientations can be permuted within each row.
  • Sequence — Five landscape photos. On a portrait page, the large image can be at the top, in the middle or at the bottom. On a landscape page, the two large images are at the top or bottom. This family is not available on a square page.

These conditions guide automatic selection; they do not prevent manual use of a template with other orientations. A square or nearly square photo, or a photo with unknown dimensions, matches neither P nor L. If such a photo is first in the remaining group, automatic mode chooses One photo.

Mosaic columns are read from left to right, and each column from top to bottom. Square mosaics and sequences arranged in rows are read from left to right, then top to bottom. Reportage has its own order: on a landscape page, a portrait on the right is the first photo, while a portrait on the left is the fourth. Photos are never reordered.

Automatic mode — 1 to 5 photos

Automatic mode first tries to place the next five photos, then four, three and two, before falling back to a single photo. It chooses the largest capacity compatible with the photo sequence and the layouts available for the page. It respects period boundaries, insertions and manual page starts defined in Plan.

For example, five landscape photos can fill a Sequence page in portrait or landscape format. On a square page, the same sequence is split into pages of two photos, two photos and one photo, because Sequence is not available there.

When several variants match, they are used in turn in menu order. Rotation is independent for each page shape and each ordered sequence of orientations. For five landscape photos on a portrait page, the layouts therefore cycle through a large image at the top, in the middle and at the bottom, then return to the top. On landscape pages, the two Sequence variants alternate.

Rotation restarts with each album recalculation: the same photos and settings produce the same choices. A page set manually from Plan does not advance the rotation. To fix a specific layout on one page, use that manual choice instead of automatic mode.
