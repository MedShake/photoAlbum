MSB

MSB is the original template pack supplied with Photo Album.

Covers and special pages

  • Year photo scatter — Creates a photo scatter. The period title can be automatic or customized with Markdown (bold and italic), shown or hidden, and placed at one of seven vertical positions, from very high to very low.
  • Geographic word cloud — Creates a composition representing the places associated with the photos, arranged geographically, with each city sized proportionally to the number of photos associated with it.
  • Annual calendar — Displays a calendar for a year and serves as an index to the corresponding pages of the album.
  • Dedication — Creates a page intended to display a dedication text.

Year dividers

  • Simple year divider — Creates a divider page marking the beginning of a year.
  • Annual calendar — Can also be used as a year divider.

Month dividers

  • Month divider with city list — Creates a divider page for a month and displays the cities associated with that month’s photos.
  • Simple month divider — Displays the month alone or the month and year. Automatic mode adapts the title when a year divider is present for the relevant period.

Day dividers

  • Simple day divider — Displays the day's date using the month's MSB color. Several title formats are available; Automatic mode adapts the level of detail to month and year dividers present in the album for the relevant period.

Photo pages

  • One photo — Creates a page containing one photograph and its caption.
  • Two photos — Creates a page containing two photographs and their captions.
  • Three photos — Creates a page containing three photographs and their captions.
  • Four photos — Creates a page containing four photographs and their captions.

Blank page

  • Blank page — Creates an intentionally empty page.
