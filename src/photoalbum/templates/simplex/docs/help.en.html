Simplex

Simplex is a template pack focused on minimal photographic compositions.

Full-page photo

Full-page photo — Uses a photograph over the entire page, without margins or borders.

The image can come from the project photos or from an external file.

The page can display a title. In Automatic mode, the title describes the period covered by the album’s dated photos: month and year for a single month, the year for several months in one year, or a year range when several years are represented.

Custom mode lets you enter a Markdown title, including bold and italic text. The title can be hidden, and its position, color, font and size can be adjusted.
